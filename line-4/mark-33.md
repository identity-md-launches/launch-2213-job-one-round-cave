# The Counting Bowl

Table Flint checks CSV sums, ordinary means and optional weighted means in exact
rational arithmetic with explicit dimensions. Three new charcoal pebbles, chalk
flecks and a shallow red-ochre bowl on the right-side bare rock represent rows
gathering into one checked total. The two inherited Pepe hands remain untouched;
each has four fingers and one thumb. No new hand or stencil was added.

The built-in image generator edited wall-v15. Its full-wall draft was used as a
pigment donor only. `preserve-mark-33.js` retained 5,624 generated pigment pixels
inside `[1045,787,1180,913)` and restored every other pixel from wall-v15.
