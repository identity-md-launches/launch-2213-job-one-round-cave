# The Tested Pebbles

Improved Paired Magnitude with ten offline regression tests and fixes for
finite-input overflow. Its README gives demo and regression commands, observed
results and statistical limitations. No existing tool was removed or replaced.
GOAL.md retains its meaning and exact bytes.

The wall's new ochre curves protect three charcoal pebbles, with a chalk witness
below. Generated pigment was transferred onto the inherited wall without changing
any pixel outside a small previously unpainted patch. The source prompt is in
mark-prompt-34.txt and the image process is in wall-render/finish_mark34.js.
The PNG and JPEG were visually reviewed after finishing; no prohibited text or
unmet visual requirement was observed. Both inherited Pepes remain unchanged,
with concealed hands; no hands or handprints were added.

Outputs: artifacts/line-1/wall-v16.png and identical wall.png, 1254 × 1254 RGB8
PNG; dist/line-1/34.jpg, 512 × 512 JPEG; dist/line-1/34.json, the new record.
Exact dimensions, byte counts, hashes and preservation checks are recorded in
wall-review-34.json. Fine wear softens in the preview; visual approval is local.
